We are not business people. We just love putting on parties and are trying to make our late night dreams come true.

We put the spotlight on brilliant artists who don't often get one: the musicians, dancers and performers working in care homes, pubs and on building sites.

There will be spaces for going feral, spaces to jump for joy, spaces for tenderness and spaces to make your own. Not to escape the world, but to leave feeling like it's a little less shit.
